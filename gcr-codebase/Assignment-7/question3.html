Linear Gradient Section

A linear gradient transitions smoothly between two or more colors along a straight directional line.

This section uses linear-gradient(135deg, #1e3c72 0%, #2a5298 50%, #00d2d3 100%). The 135-degree angle pushes colors diagonally from top-left to bottom-right, creating depth and visual interest.

Radial Gradient Section (Fixed Attachment)

A radial gradient originates from an inner focal point and radiates outwards in circular or elliptical bands.

This section uses radial-gradient(circle at center, #ff7e5f, #feb47b, #eb4d4b) combined with background-attachment: fixed;.

As you scroll up and down, notice how this card glides over the gradient while the background remains pinned to the viewport, producing a smooth parallax sensation.
