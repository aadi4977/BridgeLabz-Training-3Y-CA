2. Content-Box vs Border-Box Comparison

Both boxes have CSS width: 200px, padding: 20px, and border: 8px

content-box (Default)

Rendered Box
Formula: Width + (Padding × 2) + (Border × 2)
Total Rendered Width:
200px + 40px + 16px = 256px
The box grows larger than the declared 200px.

border-box

Rendered Box
Formula: Declared width includes padding & border.
Total Rendered Width:
Exactly 200px
Content automatically compresses to 144px to fit.
